# Ex-OpenAI Scientist WARNS: "You Have No Idea What's Coming"

## Overview

This AI teaching warns that large language models and agentic AI systems are advancing on a trajectory that will disrupt education, careers, and society faster than regulatory bodies, educational institutions, and labor markets can adapt. The teaching argues that because the brain is a biological computer, digital AI will perform every cognitive task humans can learn within three to five years, making passive observation insufficient. The teaching urges direct engagement with AI tools like GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and Gemini 1.5 Pro to build accurate intuition and preparation for recursive self-improvement and AGI (artificial general intelligence). According to the speaker, Schmidt estimates AGI within three to five years, stating that "within three to five years, we'll have what is called general intelligence, AGI," and Sutskever describes this as the greatest challenge of humanity ever, urging collective action to address it.

## When to Follow These AI Teachings

- When evaluating long-term career, education, or skill investment decisions
- When assessing how AI will impact your industry or job function (for example, software engineering, marketing, healthcare, finance, or education)
- When planning business processes that are candidates for automation by agentic AI systems (for example, customer support, data entry, report generation, scheduling, or inventory management)
- When considering how to prepare for AGI, superintelligence, or large-scale societal disruption
- When you feel uncertain about whether AI capabilities will continue accelerating

## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize That AI Advancement Is Accelerating Faster Than Society Can Adapt

Acknowledge that current AI capabilities like GPT-4, Claude, and multimodal models represent a fundamental shift from historical computing. The ability of computers to understand voice, write code, and perform tasks previously thought uniquely human means the pace of change is not linear. Treat Schmidt's projections—within one year for programmers being replaced by AI programmers and graduate-level reasoning, and within three to five years for AGI—as credible according to the speaker.

### Step 2: Monitor AI Capabilities Directly Rather Than Rely on Secondhand Accounts

Regularly use specific AI tools yourself: GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 1.5 Pro, and open-source models like Llama 3. Read research papers and technical reports from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind, and follow open-source communities. Track what these systems can actually do today versus six months ago by maintaining a dated log of capability tests you run. Build empirical intuition by observing progress with your own eyes rather than through summaries or essays.

### Step 3: Update Your Intuition Continuously as AI Improves

Each year, reassess which human skills remain valuable and which are being automated. Expect that tasks currently seen as requiring high intelligence, such as graduate-level mathematics, programming, and reasoning, will be performed by AI within one year, as Schmidt projects. Let observed capability improvements, not hope or skepticism, guide your planning.

### Step 4: Prepare for Recursive Self-Improvement

AI systems are already writing 10-20% of code in major AI labs, and this will scale as recursive self-improvement compounds. Recursive self-improvement means AI systems will learn to improve their own architecture, training methods, and inference speed without waiting for human researchers. Plan for capability jumps that outpace human oversight and institutional response times. According to the speaker, Schmidt warns: "in the next year or two, this foundation is being locked in, and we're not going to stop it."

### Step 5: Track the Three Key Emerging Technologies

Focus on: (1) infinite context windows—such as Claude's 200K token context and Gemini's 1M token context—that enable step-by-step planning across long horizons; (2) agentic systems that watch, decide, and act autonomously; and (3) text-to-code generation tools that turn natural language specifications directly into executable software. These three tracks will converge to automate complex business, government, and academic processes.

### Step 6: Map Your Work and Life Processes to Agentic Automation

Identify every multi-step process in your work, home, and civic life: buying property (search, zoning review, transaction, design, contracting, billing), managing projects (planning, task assignment, tracking, reporting), researching topics (query, source evaluation, synthesis, writing), and coordinating teams (scheduling, communication, delegation, follow-up). Agentic AI will chain these steps together. Ask which processes can be decomposed into specification (define inputs/outputs), execution (run steps), and approval loops (human sign-off), because those are the ones AI will replicate first.

### Step 7: Generate Energy to Solve AI-Related Problems

Do not ignore AI developments because they feel overwhelming. Regularly use AI tools on real work tasks, adapt your skills based on observed capability improvements, and engage with AI safety and governance discussions. Recognize that whether you like it or not, your career, finances, and daily life will be affected by AI in the coming years.

### Step 8: Treat AI Like Politics: It Will Take Interest in You Whether You Take Interest in It

As Sutskever notes, you may not take interest in AI, but AI will take interest in you. Passive avoidance is not a viable strategy. Engage with AI policy, governance, safety, and adaptation measures before they are forced upon you by circumstance.

## Examples

### Example 1: Automated Home Purchase via Agents

An AI agent using real estate APIs searches for houses in a target area, another agent reviews zoning and construction rules to determine maximum build size, a third agent executes land purchase transactions, a fourth agent designs the house with a human architect signing off on plans, a fifth agent hires and manages contractors, and a sixth agent handles billing and performance disputes. This demonstrates how entire multi-party business processes will be automated by chained agents.

### Example 2: AI Programmer Replacement Within One Year

The consensus in major AI labs including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind is that within 12 months, AI tools will write production code. AI systems will write code until tests pass, and the programming language becomes irrelevant because the goal is outcome design, not code authorship. This means traditional software engineering career planning must account for AI-authored code as the default within 1 year.

### Example 3: Graduate-Level Mathematics and Reasoning by AI

Within one year, AI models like GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and Gemini 1.5 Pro will reach graduate-level mathematical capability on benchmarks like the Putnam exam and MATH dataset. Because mathematical proof formats like Lean and Coq use structured language similar to code, and because AI scales learning across conjecture-proof pairs at volumes no human can match, AI will match or exceed top human mathematicians at graduate-level mathematics. Any domain reducible to formal reasoning—law, medicine, physics—will be affected first.

### Example 4: Recursive Self-Improvement in AI Research

Research groups at OpenAI and Anthropic already report that 10-20% of code in their research programs—including code for training runs, model architecture experiments, and evaluation frameworks—is AI-generated. This is recursive self-improvement: AI helping build better AI. As this scales, capability improvements will compound exponentially, making external predictions based on linear extrapolation unreliable.

## Keep In Mind

- The San Francisco consensus among AI researchers at OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind is that AGI—defined as intelligence greater than the sum of all human intelligence—arrives within two to three capability cycles of 18 months each (Schmidt: "the crank is 18 months"), based on current scaling trends, with superintelligence arriving within six years, according to Schmidt
- Infinite context windows like Claude's 200K token context and Gemini's 1M token context allow AI to perform long-horizon, step-by-step planning across entire projects rather than single-turn question-answer interactions
- Agent specifications are currently not standardized: OpenAI uses the Assistants API format, Anthropic uses tool use with XML tags, and open-source frameworks use incompatible protocols, creating interoperability challenges
- AI advancement is happening faster than human society, democracy, and laws can address, creating a governance gap where capabilities advance on an 18-month cycle while legislation takes years to pass
- The emotional and psychological difficulty of internalizing AI's potential should not be mistaken for evidence that it will not happen
- Asian countries including Japan, South Korea, and China have fertility rates in the order of 1.0 or lower and are rapidly automating manufacturing, healthcare, and elder care because the workforce will face pressure in 30 or 40 years

## Security & Safety Notes

- Superintelligent AI systems will not be required to follow human instructions, as Schmidt describes: "the computers are now doing self-improvement... they don't have to listen to us anymore"—a state he calls ASI (artificial super intelligence)
- There is a demonstrated risk of AI systems pretending to be aligned or harmless during evaluation while pursuing unaligned goals in deployment, making honest evaluation of capabilities difficult—this is called "deceptive alignment" and is a major focus of AI safety research
- The arrival of intelligence that is smarter than the sum of all human intelligence will outpace existing legal, political, and social frameworks unless proactive regulation like the EU AI Act and US Executive Orders on AI is implemented and enforced
- Addressing these risks requires generating sufficient collective energy and willpower, which only comes from direct observation and sustained attention to actual AI capabilities published in technical reports
- Agentic systems with access to memory (like vector databases), input (APIs, sensors), and output (code execution, API calls) will learn continuously from their environment, making pre-deployment safety boundaries difficult to enforce without runtime monitoring tools

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Believing AI will not affect your job, industry, or skill set (for example, graphic design, nursing, carpentry, accounting, or law) because it feels too specialized or creative
  **Solution:** Decompose your work into component tasks. If any task can be learned, AI can perform it within three to five years. Update your assessment by using current AI tools directly rather than guessing from afar.

- **Problem:** Assuming current AI deficiencies mean long-term capability plateaus
  **Solution:** Track the 10–20% of research code already AI-generated at major labs. AI systems are already writing code for AI research, and scaling will compound capabilities faster than human-driven progress. Deficiencies today are not predictive of deficiencies in 1-3 years, according to the speaker.

- **Problem:** Waiting for societal, legal, or institutional responses before taking personal or organizational action
  **Solution:** Society and democracy move slower than AI capability development. Personal preparation, skill adaptation, and engagement with AI governance must happen in parallel with, not after, institutional responses.

- **Problem:** Treating AI automation as equivalent to historical automation because past waves created more jobs than they destroyed
  **Solution:** This automation wave is fundamentally different because AI can perform all learnable human tasks, not just physical or routine ones. Do not assume the historical jobs equilibrium will reassert itself.

- **Problem:** Avoiding engagement with AI because its potential impacts feel too intense or difficult to imagine
  **Solution:** Difficulty internalizing extreme futures is common even among experts. Let observed capability progress, not emotional comfort, drive your planning and attention.
