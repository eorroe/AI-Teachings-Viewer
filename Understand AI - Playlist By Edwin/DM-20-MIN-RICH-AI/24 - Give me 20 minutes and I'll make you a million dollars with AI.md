# Give me 20 minutes and I'll make you a million dollars with AI

## Overview

This AI teaching provides a three-step blueprint for how to make a million dollars with AI automation without writing any code, as taught by Dan Martell. The process focuses on targeting low-competition boring markets, maintaining profit margins of at least 80%, and pre-selling solutions before building anything. It includes a 5-day pre-selling sprint and a 9-step sales system designed to get people to buy 50% of the time.

## When to Follow These AI Teachings

- When you want to start an AI automation business without coding skills
- When you need a repeatable process for finding high-paying clients
- When you want to validate demand before investing time or money into a product
- When you are selling B2B services to traditional industries with low AI adoption

## Steps

### Step 1: Target a Boring Market

Choose a growing industry with high willingness to pay for AI automation and low existing AI adoption.

1. Open ChatGPT and ask for a list of growing industries in a specific city or region where businesses pay $5,000 to $10,000 for AI automation setup.
2. Ask ChatGPT to identify growing industries whose businesses pay $5,000 to $10,000 for AI automation setup (examples include trades such as plumbing, law firms, dental or medical offices, or farming).
3. From the generated list, select one industry where AI adoption is low to focus on.
4. Conduct pain point research by reading industry reviews, studying the industry, and calling businesses to ask about bottlenecks where AI can help.
5. Position the call as market research. At the end of the conversation, reveal that you provide AI automation for businesses like theirs.
6. Conduct technology research by mapping each identified pain point to existing AI tools (for example, scheduling, data entry, lead scoring, or data monitoring).
7. Research specific tools such as Precision.co for data platforms or HelloFrank.ai for financial modeling.
8. For a plumbing business example, identify missed calls as the pain point and select a tool like your.com to handle inbound calls, qualify leads, and book appointments automatically.
9. Confirm that you are selling the setup and monthly management of the AI tool, not building the technology yourself.

### Step 2: Set Your Gross Margins

Protect profitability by keeping margins at least 80% using four controls.

1. Set a price floor by multiplying your delivery cost by five. For example, if delivery costs $2,000, charge $10,000 to achieve 80% margin.
2. Productize your offer by creating one fixed-scope automation package with a standard setup checklist, scripted sales conversation, and templated delivery workflow so you avoid custom builds.
3. Scope the outcome clearly in every agreement. If a customer requests work outside the agreed scope, present a change order with an associated fee.
4. Monitor margins continuously by tracking revenue and delivery costs in a spreadsheet or tool such as HelloFrank.ai. Set an alert to flag any project or month where margins fall below 80%.

### Step 3: Pre-Sell Your Solution

Run a 5-day AI-assisted pre-selling sprint to secure paying customers before delivering any work.

#### Day 1: Draft a One-Page Offer

1. Open ChatGPT and input the following structure:
   - Lead with a specific outcome (for example, "I help you get 10 qualified appointments over the next 30 days without you answering a single phone call").
   - Define a unique mechanism (for example, "We use our Dials to Deals system that answers, qualifies, and books appointments while you sleep").
   - Create scarcity and urgency (for example, "I only have 10 pilot spots open").
   - Include a risk reversal (for example, "Get your first appointment scheduled in the first 7 days or your money back").
2. Ask ChatGPT to write the full one-page offer using your specific details.

#### Day 2: Build a Target List

1. Open an AI tool and request a list of 100 ideal customers in your chosen city or industry on LinkedIn.
2. Instruct the AI research tool to save the list into a Google Sheet with columns for name, contact information, and LinkedIn profile URL.
3. Ask the AI to write a personalized outreach snippet for each prospect to use in cold emails, cold calls, or LinkedIn messages.

#### Day 3 and 4: Reach Out to Buyers

1. Use the following cold call script as a starting point and personalize it for each prospect:
   - "Hey, quick one, Bob. I help other plumbers get customers to buy over the phone without having to take any more calls. I have 10 pilot spots open and the investment is only $5,000. Do you want me to send the one-pager?"
2. Keep your pitch under 30 seconds: state who you help, what result you deliver, and how many pilot spots remain open. Do not deviate from those three points.
3. Optionally, use an AI tool such as your.com to make cold calls on your behalf or answer inbound qualifying calls.

#### Day 5: Close the Deal

Use the 9-step Rocket Selling System in this exact order on every sales call:

1. Setup: State the agenda and audition the buyer. Make it clear that you will determine if you help them.
2. Learn About the Customer: Ask who they are, what they sell, what they charge, and what problem they are solving.
3. Decision: Ask "Why did you decide to get on this call?" followed by "What makes this a now problem?" Let the prospect convince themselves to buy.
4. Results: Clarify what revenue or cost-saving outcome they expect, what profit target they want to hit, and what their ideal daily workflow looks like after the automation is live.
5. Reality: Learn where they are today, including team size, ownership structure, and how they currently handle calls or tasks.
6. Roadblocks: Identify what has prevented them from achieving their goals. Save their answers to use later in the sale.
7. Present the Model: Reference your named process or methodology to demonstrate that this is a mature, repeatable system.
8. Make the Offer: Match your offer directly to the pain points and roadblocks they shared earlier.
9. Ask Three Times: Ask for the deal at least three times. If they defer, use BAMF (Book A Meeting From a Meeting): propose a specific future calendar call with a date and time before ending the conversation. Do not hang up until a future calendar call is confirmed. Say: "if for whatever reason something comes up, you can't make that call, could you do me the honor of letting me know ahead of time so I can open up that spot and give it to somebody else that's looking to move forward with us."

## Best Practices

- ✅ Target boring industries with high willingness to pay and low AI adoption
- ✅ Always pre-sell before building or delivering any work
- ✅ Multiply delivery cost by five to guarantee at least 80% gross margin
- ✅ Productize your offer to avoid custom builds that destroy margins
- ✅ Write cold outreach snippets with AI using specifics from each prospect's online presence
- ✅ Ask for the deal three times in every sales conversation
- ✅ Book the next meeting before ending any sales call using BAMF
- ✅ Monitor margins with AI tools and set alerts for drops below 80%

## Keep In Mind

- Revenue is vanity. margin is for your sanity. A million in revenue with a 50% margin is only $500,000 in actual profit.
- Boring markets have less competition, and the industry-specific knowledge you build becomes a durable advantage that is hard for others to replicate quickly.
- Asking for advice instead of pitching can get you a customer.
- Pre-selling is normal in industries including software, services, coaching, and crowdfunding.
- The 9-step sales system helps even people who have never sold before get people to buy 50% of the time.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not share proprietary customer data or financial details when using AI tools for research or list building.
- Verify that any AI tool you use for calls or automation complies with local telecommunications and privacy regulations.
- Keep client agreements and scope documents clear to avoid disputes over unpaid extra work.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Building a product before confirming that customers will pay for it.
  **Solution:** Pre-sell using a one-page offer and collect payment before doing any implementation work.

- **Problem:** Margins eroding because of custom requests and scope creep.
  **Solution:** Define the scope upfront and charge change-order fees for any work outside the original agreement.

- **Problem:** Losing deals because you only ask for the sale once.
  **Solution:** Ask for the deal three times and address objections each time before moving on.

- **Problem:** No-shows and ghosting after sales calls.
  **Solution:** Always book the next meeting before ending the call and ask the prospect to notify you in advance if they need to reschedule.

- **Problem:** Custom builds for every client that destroy profitability.
  **Solution:** Productize the offer into a single repeatable automation that can be delivered the same way every time.
