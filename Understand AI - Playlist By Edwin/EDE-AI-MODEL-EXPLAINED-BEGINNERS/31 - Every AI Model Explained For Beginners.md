# Every AI Model Explained For Beginners

## Overview

This AI teaching introduces eight AI models available today: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Llama, Perplexity, Grok, and Midjourney. For each model, it explains what it does, which company develops it, and what tasks it handles best—whether that is writing, coding, research, image generation, or accessing real-time information.

## When to Follow These AI Teachings

- When you are new to AI and need a clear comparison of these eight AI models
- When you need to choose the right AI tool for a specific task such as writing, coding, research, or image generation
- When you want to understand the differences between consumer AI assistants and open-source foundation models
- When you need real-time information, multimodal input support, or long-document analysis capabilities

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand ChatGPT and Its Generations

ChatGPT is one of the most widely used AI assistants in the world. It has gone through generations including GPT-3, GPT-3.5, GPT-4, GPT-4 Turbo, GPT-4o, GPT-5, and GPT-5.2, each improving intelligence, reasoning, and reliability.

- **GPT-3.5**: Became popular because it writes essays, generates stories, answers questions, and writes basic code in seconds.
- **GPT-4**: Improved reasoning compared to GPT-3, handled complex prompts better, solved difficult logic problems, and made fewer obvious mistakes.
- **GPT-4 Turbo**: Focused on speed and efficiency improvements.
- **GPT-4o**: Introduced multimodal capabilities, meaning it can understand text, images, and voice. You can upload a photo and ask it to analyze it.
- **GPT-5**: Improved multi-step logic, stronger at handling complicated instructions without losing track of context, and gave more structured answers.
- **GPT-5.2**: Builds on everything before it with improved contextual memory for long conversations, and is stronger in technical domains like math, programming, and business analysis.

### Step 2: Use Gemini for Google Ecosystem Integration

Gemini is developed by Google and designed to integrate directly into Google's ecosystem, including Gmail, Google Docs, Sheets, and Google Search.

- One of Gemini's biggest strengths is its connection to real-time information through Google's infrastructure, allowing it to access and process web data for research and current events.
- Gemini handles multimodal input, meaning it can process text, images, and other data types together. Upload a photo and ask Gemini to analyze it or extract information from it.
- Gemini has deep integration into Android devices, allowing users to interact with AI naturally through voice and text directly from their smartphones.
- Choose Gemini when your daily life revolves around Google products and you need an AI assistant embedded in that ecosystem.

### Step 3: Use Claude for Long-Form Writing and Document Analysis

Claude is developed by Anthropic. The video states that Claude is "widely known for focusing on safety and thoughtful responses." The company behind Claude places a strong emphasis on alignment and responsible AI behavior.

- According to the video, Claude delivers calm and organized responses for detailed essays, structured reports, or carefully explained concepts.
- Claude can process long documents such as research papers, contracts, or books at once, making it helpful for analyzing research papers, contracts, or books.
- Claude is used by people who value careful reasoning and long thoughtful answers.
- Choose Claude when you need to analyze long documents, write structured reports, or get reasoned explanations.

### Step 4: Use DeepSeek for Reasoning and Coding Tasks

According to the video, DeepSeek gained attention because of its performance in reasoning and coding tasks.

- DeepSeek focuses heavily on logical problem-solving and technical applications. Developers test DeepSeek against other models in programming challenges.
- DeepSeek delivers performance without requiring significant computational resources compared to some other large models.
- DeepSeek handles structured reasoning problems well and provides step-by-step solutions.
- Choose DeepSeek when you are a student studying math or computer science, or when you need step-by-step solutions to technical problems.

### Step 5: Use Llama as an Open-Source Foundation Model

Llama is developed by Meta and serves primarily as a foundation model rather than a public-facing assistant.

- Developers and researchers can build their own AI systems on top of Llama. The video states that it is "commonly used in open-source AI projects and custom applications."
- Llama's biggest contribution is flexibility. Because it is accessible to developers, it allows experimentation and innovation in healthcare, education, finance, and creative industries.
- Llama models have been fine-tuned into specialized tools. Some chatbots and AI platforms you see online use variations of Llama.
- Universities and startups can use Llama as a starting point for building specialized AI systems.
- Choose Llama when you are a developer or researcher looking to build custom AI applications or experiment with open-source models.

### Step 6: Use Perplexity for Research and Fact-Checking

Perplexity is an AI-powered search engine.

- Unlike traditional chatbots that simply generate answers, Perplexity emphasizes providing answers with citations and sources. When you ask a question, Perplexity shows references from websites so you can verify the information.
- Perplexity combines conversational AI with web browsing capabilities, feeling like a smarter and more interactive version of search engines.
- Perplexity can quickly summarize articles, news updates, and online discussions in a clear format.
- Students and professionals use Perplexity when they need quick overviews of topics with reliable sources attached.
- Choose Perplexity when you need research with verifiable sources, fact-checking, or topic summarization.

### Step 7: Use Grok for Real-Time Social and Trend Analysis

Grok is developed by XAI (the company behind the X social platform) and is closely integrated with the X platform (formerly Twitter).

- Grok is designed to provide information from social media and trending discussions.
- According to the video, one of Grok's defining characteristics is personality. It has a bold, edgy tone compared to other AI models.
- Because it is integrated into a social platform, Grok can respond to live discussions and current trends in real time, making it useful for following breaking news or viral topics.
- Grok can summarize high-volume social discussions into clear overviews. Instead of manually scrolling through hundreds of posts, users can ask Grok to explain what is happening.
- Grok benefits from being directly connected to live data streams, reacting faster to new developments than models that rely only on static training data.
- Choose Grok when you need to follow breaking news, analyze viral topics, or understand live social media discussions.

### Step 8: Use Midjourney for Image Generation

Midjourney is an image generation tool rather than a text-based chatbot. It converts text prompts into visual artwork instead of answering questions or writing text.

- When you type a description into Midjourney, it creates images such as realistic portraits, futuristic cities, and fantasy landscapes based on your prompt.
- Midjourney is widely used by designers, artists, and content creators who need visuals quickly.
- According to the video, artistic quality is one of Midjourney's biggest strengths.
- Midjourney works by interpreting your text prompt as visual concepts, lighting styles, textures, and composition ideas, then combining them into a final image. The more detailed your prompt is, the more precise the result becomes, producing outputs like realistic portraits, futuristic cities, and fantasy landscapes.
- Midjourney allows users to refine and upscale images, meaning you can start with a general idea and slowly turn it into something refined and upscaled.
- Choose Midjourney when you need to generate high-quality images from text descriptions.

## Examples

### Example 1: Choosing an AI Model for Coding

If you are a computer science student working on a programming assignment, use DeepSeek to get step-by-step solutions and logical explanations. For writing documentation or explaining your code, use Claude for clear long-form writing. For quick coding help and multi-step logic problems, use GPT-5.2 which is stronger in programming and technical domains.

### Example 2: Choosing an AI Model for Research

If you are a student writing a research paper, use Perplexity to find sources with citations and verify information. Use Claude to analyze long research papers or contracts by uploading large documents. Use Gemini to access real-time web data for current events through Google Search integration.

### Example 3: Choosing an AI Model for Image Creation

If you are a designer or content creator who needs a visual for a project, use Midjourney. Write a detailed text prompt describing the scene, lighting style, textures, and composition. Refine and upscale the generated image until it matches your vision.

### Example 4: Choosing an AI Model for Social Media Monitoring

If you want to understand a trending topic or breaking news on social media, use Grok. Ask Grok to summarize the discussion, explain what is happening, and provide context from live public conversations. Grok reacts faster to new developments than models relying only on static training data.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Match the AI model to your specific task. Use ChatGPT for general assistance, Claude for long-form writing, DeepSeek for coding and reasoning, and Perplexity for research.
- ✅ Provide detailed prompts when using Midjourney. The more specific your description of visual concepts, lighting, textures, and composition, the better your generated image will be.
- ✅ Use Gemini when you need real-time information or integration with Google products like Gmail, Docs, and Sheets.
- ✅ Upload large documents to Claude for analysis of long-form content such as research papers, contracts, or books.
- ✅ Use Perplexity instead of traditional chatbots when you need verifiable sources and citations for your research.
- ✅ Choose Llama if you are a developer or researcher who wants to build custom AI systems on an open-source foundation.
- ❌ Do not rely on a single AI model for all tasks. ChatGPT and Claude excel at writing; DeepSeek and GPT-5.2 are strongest for coding and reasoning; Perplexity is built for research with citations; Midjourney is designed exclusively for image generation.
- ❌ Do not use Midjourney for text-based tasks. It is designed exclusively for image generation.
- ❌ Do not assume all AI models have the same knowledge cutoff or real-time data access. Check which models are connected to live data streams.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Claude is developed by Anthropic. The company behind Claude places a strong emphasis on alignment and responsible AI behavior, and it is designed to produce safe and thoughtful responses.
- When using any AI model for sensitive tasks such as analyzing contracts, legal documents, or personal data, verify the output independently and do not rely solely on the AI's interpretation.
- AI models can make mistakes confidently. Always fact-check important information, especially when using models without built-in citation features.
- When using AI-generated images from Midjourney, be aware of potential copyright and licensing considerations for commercial use.
- Do not input sensitive personal information, passwords, or confidential data into any AI model unless you understand the platform's data handling policies.
- Models with real-time data access, such as Gemini and Grok, process current events and social media data. Exercise critical thinking when evaluating AI-generated summaries of live discussions.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Using an AI model that is not suited for your specific task.
  **Solution:** Match the model to the task. Use DeepSeek or GPT-5.2 for coding, Claude for long-form writing, Perplexity for research with citations, and Midjourney for image generation.

- **Problem:** Expecting all AI models to have the same capabilities.
  **Solution:** Understand that each model has distinct capabilities. ChatGPT and Claude excel at writing and long-form analysis. DeepSeek and GPT-5.2 are strongest for coding and math. Perplexity provides citations with every answer. Midjourney generates images but cannot write or reason in text. Not all models support multimodal input, real-time data, or long-document processing.

- **Problem:** Using vague prompts with Midjourney and getting low-quality images.
  **Solution:** Write detailed prompts that specify visual concepts, lighting styles, textures, and composition. Refine and upscale images iteratively.

- **Problem:** Relying on a chatbot without citations for fact-checking and research.
  **Solution:** Use Perplexity instead, as it provides sources and references with every answer so you can verify the information.

- **Problem:** Assuming open-source models like Llama are ready-to-use consumer chatbots.
  **Solution:** Llama is a foundation model for developers. If you want a polished chatbot experience, use ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini instead.

- **Problem:** Ignoring the generation differences within ChatGPT and using an older version.
  **Solution:** Use the latest generation (GPT-5.2) for the best contextual memory, technical domain performance, and multi-step reasoning capabilities.

## Glossary

- **Alignment**: The process of training an AI model to behave in ways that are safe, ethical, and aligned with human values.
- **Foundation model**: A base AI model that can be adapted or fine-tuned for specific tasks, rather than being a ready-to-use consumer product.
- **Multimodal capabilities**: The ability of an AI model to understand and process three types of input, such as text, images, and voice.
- **Contextual memory**: The ability of an AI model to remember and reference earlier parts of a conversation, maintaining coherence over long interactions.
