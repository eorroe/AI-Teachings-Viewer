Let's start with the biggest name, Chat
GPT. It is one of the most widely used
AI assistant in the world. However, many
people do not realize that Chat GPT has
gone through multiple generations, and
each generation significantly improved
intelligence, reasoning, and
reliability. The GPT-3
era is what made AI mainstream. GPT-3.5
became the version that went viral
because it could suddenly write essays,
generate stories, answer questions, and
even write basic code in seconds.
However, GPT-3 models had limitations.
They often made mistakes confidently.
They struggled with complex math. The
GPT-4 generation was a major leap
forward in intelligence. GPT-4 improved
reasoning dramatically compared to
GPT-3. It handled complex prompts
better, solved more difficult logic
problems, and made fewer obvious
mistakes. Then came GPT-4 Turbo. Turbo
focused on speed and efficiency. After
that, GPT-4o introduced multimodal
capabilities. This meant it could
understand text, images, and voice more
naturally. You could upload a photo and
ask it to analyze it. GPT-5 improved
multi-step logic. It became stronger at
handling complicated instructions
without losing track of context. It
reduced logical errors and gave more
structured answers. Now we have GPT-5.2,
which builds on everything before it.
GPT-5.2
improves contextual memory, making long
conversations more coherent. It is
stronger in technical domains like math,
programming, and business analysis.
