# 21 NEW Things You Should Be Using AI For...

## Overview

This AI teaching provides a curated list of AI use cases the speaker has actually been using, demonstrated with real examples for everyday life and work, showing how to use AI assistants for shopping, productivity, entertainment, and problem-solving. It shows specific prompts and workflows for tasks like product research, calendar management, recipe substitutions, travel planning, video analysis, and creative projects, with concrete examples of interacting with AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Sora, Notebook LM, and calendar, Spotify, and Airbnb connectors.

## When to Follow These AI Teachings

- When you need to research products, compare options, or determine the best time to make a purchase
- When troubleshooting tech issues, appliances, or household problems
- When planning travel, finding accommodations, or organizing your schedule
- When working with recipes, cooking, or identifying unknown items like bugs or plants
- When doing real estate research for leasing, buying, or renting
- When creating or analyzing video content, including translation, prompting, and trend analysis
- When generating creative visuals like dream home designs, age transformations, or co-drawing

## Steps

### Step 1: Shop Smarter With AI Product Comparisons

When you need a product comparable to one you already know, ask the AI to compare based on specific criteria. For example, ask "What's a comparable vacuum to Dyson cordless?" and specify what matters to you: battery run time, suction power, floor head compatibility, filtration, weight, and versatility. Provide your budget and main flooring type so the AI can narrow down to one to two best fits.

### Step 2: Time Your Purchases With AI

Before buying any product, especially from companies with frequent refreshes like Apple, ask the AI: "When was the last model released? How big a step increase was it from the last generation? When do you expect another new model and when will it be released?" The AI can provide a recommendation on whether now is a good time to buy and can also check for expected discounts or special deals. The speaker says: "The best time to buy any product is when the most recent refresh occurs and that is especially true with Apple."

### Step 3: Troubleshoot Tech Issues Collaboratively

Describe the problem to the AI without needing a photo first. For example, "Power and ink lights won't stop flashing on Epson NX127 printer." The AI can look up manuals and provide troubleshooting steps. Continue the conversation with follow-up results like "I reinserted the cartridges and now both lights are solid" so the AI can refine its diagnosis and help you solve the problem.

### Step 4: Adapt Recipes With Ingredient Substitutions

Get a recipe from the AI, then tell it which ingredients you do not have and ask for replacements. For example, say "I don't have white onions, only yellow. Will that work?" The AI can confirm if the substitute works and provide adjustments like "chop them finely and add more lime or salt to balance." You can also upload a photo of your fridge or cabinets and ask what else can be substituted.

### Step 5: Identify Unknown Bugs or Animals

Take a clear photo of the unknown creature and upload it to the AI with the question "What bug is this?" The AI can identify it, explain when and why it appears (such as during swarming season, usually after rain or in warm weather), and provide context to help you assess whether the situation is a concern. The speaker says: "As soon as I heard termite, I got very worried. But it did say it occurs after rain and it did just rain. So, it's okay."

### Step 6: Research Real Estate and Leasing

Ask the AI for the leasing price of a specific address if pricing is not published publicly. Request comparable properties ("comps") from the last 6 months in that area. Ask for average price per square foot in different neighborhoods to get a market sense for office leasing, house renting, or home buying.

### Step 7: Create Document Templates From Past Examples

Upload previous similarly formatted documents to the AI and instruct it to create a new template. For example, say "Here are three similarly formatted documents where I track work. I want to create a new version formatted around a new approach. Please make the new version only cover the sections I specify." The AI can generate a reusable template for future documents.

### Step 8: Negotiate With Your Calendar Using AI

Ask the AI to add specific time blocks to your calendar. For example, say "Please add two 1-hour chunks, one in the morning and one in the afternoon, that fit my calendar availability for heads-down work time." If the AI has a calendar connector, it can add events directly. If not, ask it to create clickable links with all relevant event information—such as event name, busy periods, and available time slots—that you can use to add the event to Google Calendar.

### Step 9: Search Travel Accommodations Through AI

Connect the AI to services like Airbnb and search with detailed criteria. For example, say "Find five options for Airbnbs in Hawaii the first week of January starting January 2nd. I want options that are superhosts, have on-site parking, and ocean views." Refine your search through follow-up messages instead of manual browsing.

### Step 10: Plan Routes and Research Destinations With Maps

Ask the AI to show a route between two locations, then add destination research. For example, say "Show me a route from SF to Oakland. I need to find a Thai restaurant near my destination. Can you conduct research, make suggestions, and plot the options on a map?" The AI can return restaurant options with atmosphere ratings, addresses, websites, photos, and an interactive map.

### Step 11: Control Music and Media With AI

Connect the AI to Spotify and control playback or create playlists with natural language. Say "Next Spotify song" to skip tracks. Ask for playlists based on mood or activity, such as "a lo-fi playlist with lo-fi music better for morning work." Approve the connector prompt and the AI can start playing the selected content.

### Step 12: Generate Perfect Sora Prompts With a Custom GPT

Create a custom GPT that drafts optimal prompts for Sora from rough descriptions. Feed the GPT context such as camera angles, lighting, subject details, motion descriptors, and aspect ratio, then give it a simple idea like "I want to make a prompt of Matthew Berman and Jonah Lipsitz dancing in a field giggling with balloons." The GPT can return a detailed Sora prompt with all required elements.

### Step 13: Control Your Likeness Usage in Sora

When generating videos with your likeness in Sora, instruct the AI to include promotions or specific messages. For example, say "Promote forwardfuture.ai somewhere in the video." This lets you get something out of it while allowing others to use it.

### Step 14: Visualize Your Dream Home

Use Gemini with image generation to design a dream home. Start with a detailed prompt like "Prompt a modern home perched on a rugged seaside cliff at golden hour with vast panoramic ocean views." Then ask follow-up questions for specific views: "Show me the view directly out of the window from the living room," "What about the kitchen?" "Is there a front yard?" "Main bedroom," "main bathroom," "downstairs vault."

### Step 15: Create a Real Estate Video Tour

Take the images generated from your dream home prompts and use a video generation feature like Google's Flow to create a real estate style video. Upload all images, set the aspect ratio, number of outputs, and model version, then add the prompt "no voiceover at the end." The AI can tie the images together with natural cuts and music into a polished property tour.

### Step 16: See Yourself at Any Age

Upload a headshot photo and ask the AI to show you at a different age. For example, say "Make me an image of this man as a young child" or "Make me an image of this man as a 99-year-old." The AI can generate a realistic image of you at that age.

### Step 17: Co-Draw With AI

Use Google AI Studio's Gemini Co-drawing feature to create and refine images interactively. Start drawing like in Microsoft Paint, then instruct the AI to clean up or modify your drawing in real time. For example, say "Make the head more circular and add ears," then continue drawing and ask it to "make the hands and feet look more like hands and feet." The AI maintains the style while improving details.

### Step 18: Analyze Tweet Trends With Grok

Use Grok to conduct trend analysis on X/Twitter over time. For example, say "Conduct a tweet trend analysis from last 6 months compared to previous 6 months regarding mentions of quantum computing breakthroughs. Include a graph and list of tweet examples." The AI can research posts across two time periods and themes, track how those themes trend over time, pull out example tweets, and provide sentiment analysis.

### Step 19: Animate a Picture Book With Notebook LM

Upload any document, PDF, book, or article to Notebook LM. It can create an audio podcast with two AI hosts discussing the topic. To create a video overview, select a video style such as documentary, tutorial, or summary, explicitly state what you want the AI hosts to focus on, and generate the video. The output is a 6.5-minute animated video with visuals explaining the content.

### Step 20: Analyze Videos With AI

Use ChatGPT Atlas in agent mode or Gemini 2.5 Pro to watch entire videos and answer specific questions. In ChatGPT Atlas, give the AI a video and ask it to find specific moments, such as "Please watch this movie and find moments in which Sam Altman laughs or looks like he is about to laugh. Give me a list." In Gemini 2.5 Pro, paste a video directly into Google AI Studio and ask any question about details throughout the video, since it watches frame by frame rather than just sampling frames.

### Step 21: Translate Videos to Other Languages

Use HeyGen on Replicate to translate videos into other languages with synced mouth movements. Upload the original video and select the target language. The AI can translate the voice and sync the mouth movements to the new language, creating a natural viewing experience in the target language.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Be specific with your requests. Include budget, quantity, dates, and criteria to get better results.
- ✅ Work back and forth with the AI. Provide follow-up results from your troubleshooting attempts so it can refine its advice.
- ✅ Use connectors when you have configured them to integrate AI directly with your calendar, Spotify, Airbnb, and other services.
- ✅ Upload photos or documents when relevant. Visual and document context improves AI accuracy.
- ✅ Create custom GPTs for repeated tasks like Sora prompting to get perfect Sora prompts every time.
- ✅ Use Grok for social media trend analysis and sentiment tracking.
- ✅ Use Notebook LM to turn dense documents into engaging audio or video overviews.
- ❌ Do not assume the AI has access to real-time data without verifying. Calendar, Spotify, and Airbnb connectors can fail without warning, so have a workaround ready.
- ❌ Do not skip follow-up messages. Iterative conversation improves results.

## Keep In Mind

- Calendar, Spotify, and Airbnb connectors can fail without warning even when properly configured. If the AI says it does not have access to a service you connected, ask it to create a workaround like a clickable link with event details.
- ChatGPT Atlas and agent mode require a paid account, while Gemini 2.5 Pro in Google AI Studio can analyze full videos for free.
- Video generation tools like Flow and Sora still produce quality issues such as morphing artifacts between scenes. Review outputs carefully before finalizing.
- AI-generated real estate prices, sales predictions, and product recommendations are estimates. Verify critical information independently.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not upload sensitive personal documents, financial records, or private photos to AI tools.
- When using likeness features in Sora or other video tools, understand how your image is used by others and set appropriate controls.
- Be cautious when connecting AI tools to accounts with access to personal data, such as Google Calendar, email, or financial services.
- Verify AI-generated translations, medical advice, or safety-critical information with a human before acting on it.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** The AI calendar connector says it does not have access even though you connected it.
  **Solution:** Ask the AI to create a link with all relevant event information that you can click to add the event directly to your calendar.

- **Problem:** AI-generated video content has morphing artifacts between scenes.
  **Solution:** Regenerate with adjusted prompts, use higher-quality models, or manually edit the final output.

- **Problem:** AI gives generic shopping advice that doesn't incorporate specific constraints like budget, flooring type, and quality requirements.
  **Solution:** Provide specific constraints like budget, use case, flooring type, and quality requirements to get tailored recommendations.

- **Problem:** AI cannot access unpublished real estate data, such as pricing not published publicly.
  **Solution:** Ask the AI to pull comparable properties and market averages instead of expecting exact unpublished figures.

## Glossary

- **Agent mode**: A mode in ChatGPT Atlas where the AI watches entire videos and performs tasks like finding specific moments or answering detailed questions about video content.
