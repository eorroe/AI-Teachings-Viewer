# Every Google Gemini 3 Pro Feature You'll NEED in 2026

## Overview

This AI teaching guides you through using Google Gemini 3 Pro's full multimodal feature set. Multimodal means the model can understand and generate text, images, audio, video, PDFs (Portable Document Format), and entire code bases. This teaching shows you how to access and use these features step-by-step to analyze videos, generate images with accurate text for thumbnails and designs, build interactive learning tools, and create podcasts from documents.

## When to Follow These AI Teachings

- When you need to analyze large documents, research reports, or PDFs and extract the main argument, methodology, and limitations
- When you want to analyze images, screenshots, business cards, or dashboards and extract specific fields such as names, job titles, companies, phone numbers, emails, and websites
- When you need to generate images with accurate, readable text for thumbnails, posters, or designs
- When you want to create short videos, animate static images, or blend reference images
- When you need to build interactive learning tools where you can tweak inputs and see results in real time, physics simulations, or educational visualizations
- When you want hands-free brainstorming or real-time visual reasoning through voice or live camera mode
- When you want to turn documents into podcast-style audio discussions

## Steps

### Step 1: Select the Model and Thinking Level

1. Log into Gemini at gemini.google.com or open the Gemini app.
2. Click the model picker at the top of the interface.
3. Select "thinking" to access Gemini 3 Pro.
4. Choose a thinking level:
   - Low thinking for quick, surface-level responses.
   - High thinking for complex reasoning, long-context understanding, and agentic tasks such as planning, executing, and completing multi-step workflows. The model will pause to think before responding.

### Step 2: Perform Document and Long-Context Analysis

1. Open Gemini in the web interface.
2. Click the attachment/upload icon and upload a PDF, research report, or document.
3. Type a prompt such as: "Summarize this document and answer the following questions: What is the main argument, what methodology was used, and what are the key limitations?"
4. Review the output for the main argument, the methodology used, the key limitations, and any specific answers.
5. For documents up to 30 pages, ensure high thinking is selected so the model processes the full 2 million token context window (the amount of information the model can process at once).

### Step 3: Analyze Images, Screenshots, and Dashboards

1. Upload an image by clicking the attachment/upload icon.
2. Type a specific extraction prompt. Example: "Extract the name, job title, company, phone number, email, and website from this business card."
3. For dashboards or charts, use prompts like: "Analyze this dashboard, identify key metrics, identify data points that deviate from expected values or trends, and suggest next steps."
4. Review the structured output and verify accuracy against the image.

### Step 4: Analyze Video Content

1. Upload a video file.
2. Type a prompt such as: "Break this video into chapters, highlight notable sections or highlights, identify the emotions shown, and suggest how to improve the pacing."
3. Review the chapter breakdown, insights, and pacing notes.
4. Use this output for video editing, content repurposing, or editorial review.

### Step 5: Use Extended AI Mode in Google Search

1. Open Google Search and ensure AI Mode is enabled.
2. Toggle on extended thinking in the AI Mode settings.
3. Type a complex query such as: "How does quantum entanglement work?" or "Visualize how compound interest grows over 20 years with $10,000 at a 7% annual return."
4. Review the curated answer, the model's analysis and insights, and any generated visuals or breakdowns.

### Step 6: Generate Educational Visuals and Simulations

1. In Gemini, request a visual explanation: "Create a visual explanation of projectile motion showing the curved path, velocity components, and key stages."
2. Request accompanying code: "Write a Python script with matplotlib animation that simulates projectile motion, calculates time of flight and maximum height, and plots the trajectory."
3. Run the generated script or ask Gemini to turn it into an application with adjustable parameters that updates results in real time.
4. Iterate by requesting additions such as air resistance or user input controls.

### Step 7: Use Voice Mode for Hands-Free Brainstorming

1. Open the Gemini mobile app.
2. Tap the microphone icon to enable voice mode.
3. Speak your request. Example: "Suggest five YouTube title options about AI automation tools, a hook that grabs attention in the first 10 seconds, and a content structure for a 15-minute video."
4. Review the text response generated from your voice input.

### Step 8: Use Live Mode for Real-Time Visual Reasoning

1. Open the Gemini mobile app.
2. Enable live mode and grant camera or screen sharing permissions.
3. Point the camera at a physical object, drawing, or screen, or share your screen.
4. Ask questions in real time. Example: "Read this flowchart I drew and tell me if there are any logical errors or missing steps."
5. Review the real-time suggestions and adjust your work based on the feedback.

### Step 9: Generate Images with Gemini's Image Generation

1. In Gemini, type an image generation prompt. Example: "Create a YouTube thumbnail with bold text that says AI tools 2025 on a tech background with blue and purple gradients."
2. Review the output. If text rendering is inaccurate, regenerate with a prompt emphasizing accurate, readable text without letter distortion.
3. To edit an existing image, upload it and type an editing prompt. Example: "Transform this sunny street scene into a moody cinematic night scene with neon reflections on wet pavement."
4. To blend reference images, upload up to 14 reference images and instruct Gemini's image generation to combine them into one cohesive scene.

### Step 10: Generate Videos with Veo 3.1

1. In Gemini, type a video generation prompt. Example: "Generate a video of two people having a conversation in a busy coffee shop with natural dialogue about their weekend plans."
2. Review the 8-second output at 720p or 1080p with native synchronized audio.
3. To animate a static image, upload the image and type an image-to-video prompt. Example: "Animate this smartphone image rotating slowly on a dark surface with neon lights reflecting off the screen and the camera zooming in."
4. To transition between two images, upload both images and type a transition prompt. Example: "Create a seamless transition from image 1 to image 2 where the hiker's movement from standing still to raising their arms looks natural, with hair and clothes moving in the breeze as sunlight increases."
5. Upload the generated video to Veo 3.1 if you need to extend or refine it further.

### Step 11: Turn Documents into Podcasts with Notebook LM

1. Open Notebook LM and create a new notebook.
2. Upload a PDF, research paper, article, or set of notes.
3. Click "Generate Audio Overview."
4. Notebook LM will read the entire document, identify main arguments, and create a conversational podcast between two AI hosts.
5. Listen to the audio overview and use it for review, study, or content summarization.

## Examples

### Example 1: Investment Comparison with High Thinking

**Input prompt:** "You have $5,000 to invest. Option A gives you 8% return per year. Option B gives you 12% return but has a $200 yearly fee. Which one makes more money after 5 years?"

**Process:** Select Gemini 3 Pro with high thinking enabled. The model calculates net returns for both options, factors in the fee for Option B, compares the totals, and presents the winner with the actual numbers.

**Output:** A side-by-side comparison showing the final value of both options and a clear recommendation.

### Example 2: Visual and Code-Based Physics Learning

**Input prompt:** "Create a visual explanation of projectile motion and then write a Python script with matplotlib animation that simulates it."

**Process:** Request the visual explanation first. Review the diagrams showing the curved path, horizontal and vertical velocity components, launch angle, apex, and landing. Then request the Python code. Review the script for correct physics equations, time of flight, maximum height, maximum range, and trajectory plotting.

**Output:** Diagrams showing the curved path, horizontal and vertical velocity components, launch angle, apex, and landing, plus a runnable Python script that animates projectile motion. You can further request additions like air resistance or interactive input sliders.

### Example 3: Image-to-Video Product Ad

**Input prompt:** "Animate this image of a sleek smartphone rotating slowly on a dark surface with neon lights reflecting off the screen. Camera slowly zooms in."

**Process:** Generate the smartphone image using Gemini's image generation, then upload it to Veo 3.1 with the animation prompt.

**Output:** An 8-second video showing the product rotating smoothly, neon reflections pulsing, and the camera zooming in, creating a full video ad in under three minutes.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Always select high thinking mode when working with calculations, 30-page PDFs or research papers, or multi-step reasoning tasks.
- ✅ Use prompts that specify text content, colors, composition, and style when generating images.
- ✅ Verify extracted data from images against the original file, especially for small text.
- ✅ Iterate on generated code by requesting specific additions such as air resistance or interactive input controls rather than rewriting from scratch.
- ✅ Use Notebook LM for podcast-style reviews of 50-page research papers instead of reading them manually.
- ✅ Enable AI Mode with extended thinking in Google Search for multi-part or advanced queries such as "How does quantum entanglement work?" or "Visualize how compound interest grows over 20 years with $10,000 at a 7% annual return."
- ❌ Do not rely on low thinking mode for financial calculations or document analysis requiring precision.
- ❌ Do not upload sensitive personal documents to Notebook LM or Gemini if you do not want them processed by Google's AI.
- ❌ Do not assume generated images are ready for professional print without verifying text legibility and resolution.
- ❌ Do not use generated audio or video in production without checking for artifacts, lip-sync issues, or audio glitches.

## Keep In Mind

- Gemini 3 Pro has a 2 million token context window (the amount of information the model can process at once), which allows it to process documents up to 30 pages that other tools may ignore or truncate.
- The model outperforms GPT 5.1, Claude Sonnet 4.5, and its own predecessor on advanced reasoning benchmarks (standardized tests including Humanity's Last Exam for reasoning, GPQA Diamond for graduate-level knowledge, and Screenspot Pro for UI understanding), scoring 37.5% on Humanity's Last Exam, 91.9% on GPQA Diamond, and 72.7% on Screenspot Pro.
- Gemini's image generation is specifically optimized for images with accurate, readable text without letter distortion—meaning generated text contains no distorted letters and displays clean, professional typography.
- Veo 3.1 generates videos up to 8 seconds long at 720p or 1080p with native synchronized audio.
- Notebook LM generates podcast-style audio overviews from your documents by creating a two-host conversation that breaks down complex ideas into plain language.
- Live mode provides real-time visual reasoning through your phone's camera or screen, identifying objects, text, layouts, and potential issues as they appear.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not upload confidential, personal, or sensitive documents to Gemini or Notebook LM unless you understand and accept Google's data processing policies.
- Review generated images and videos before using them publicly, as generated images and videos may contain visual artifacts, and generated text may reflect biases or inaccuracies.
- Do not use Gemini-generated code in production environments without independent review and testing.
- Be cautious when relying on Gemini for legal, medical, or financial decisions; always verify outputs with qualified professionals.
- If you use voice mode or live mode in public spaces, be aware that Google records or processes your conversations and visual data.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Generated images have distorted or unreadable text.
  **Solution:** Explicitly request accurate, readable text without letter distortion in your prompt and regenerate if the first output is unclear. In the YouTube thumbnail test with 'AI tools 2025' text, the generated image contained no distorted letters and displayed clean, professional typography—meaning no letter distortion and readable text formatted for professional use.
- **Problem:** Long documents are summarized poorly or key sections are missed.
  **Solution:** Use high thinking mode and break your request into targeted questions like "What is the main argument, what methodology was used, and what are the key limitations?" rather than asking for a broad summary.
- **Problem:** Generated videos have lip-sync or audio issues.
  **Solution:** Review the output carefully and regenerate with a prompt specifying conversational speech that matches the scene context and synchronized audio. Veo 3.1 handles this well, but complex scenes may need additional attempts.
- **Problem:** Extracted data from business cards or forms contains errors.
  **Solution:** Cross-check extracted fields against the original image, especially for small text or handwritten content.
- **Problem:** Generated code does not run or does not match the intended behavior.
  **Solution:** Ask Gemini to explain the code line by line, verify the physics or logic yourself, and request incremental changes rather than major rewrites.
