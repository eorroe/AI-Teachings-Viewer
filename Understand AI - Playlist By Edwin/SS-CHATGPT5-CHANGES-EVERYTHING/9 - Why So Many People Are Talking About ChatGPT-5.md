## Steps

### Step 1: Use GPT-5's router model instead of manually choosing a model

Describe the outcome you want rather than selecting between GPT-3.5, GPT-4, GPT-4o, or earlier variants. GPT-5 routes your request to the right underlying model automatically. This removes model-selection overhead and makes 700 million free users immediately productive. Treat the router as your default interface to AI, not a specific model version. Router-model architecture means the system automatically selects the appropriate underlying model for each request rather than requiring the user to choose.

### Step 2: Connect AI to your email and calendar and assign a real task

Integrate your AI tool with your email and calendar systems. Issue a concrete instruction such as: "Find five people I should reconnect with. Book time for calls, research them, and prep what I should say." This transforms the AI from a passive chatbot into a chief-of-staff that reads context, proposes next steps, and takes small autonomous actions.

### Step 3: Start your project now using today's AI pricing

According to the speaker, the cost of using AI has dropped 98%, from $75 per million words on GPT-4.5 to $1.25 per million words on GPT-5. ### Step 4: Get fluent with AI by using it daily on real work problems

Choose one capable AI tool, such as GPT-5, Gemini 2.5, Grok 4, Claude 4.1, or DeepSeek, and use it every day to solve actual work tasks. Do not limit usage to casual experimentation. Daily practice on real problems builds the practical intuition and speed that will matter most as AI becomes embedded in every workflow.

### Step 5: Learn the limits by pushing AI to the edge of its capabilities

Intentionally test AI systems on tasks spanning multi-step reasoning, factual lookup, and creative generation. Document what they handle well and where they fail, hallucinate, or produce inaccurate output. That boundary intuition is as valuable as knowing the strengths, because it tells you when to delegate, when to verify, and when human judgment must take over.
