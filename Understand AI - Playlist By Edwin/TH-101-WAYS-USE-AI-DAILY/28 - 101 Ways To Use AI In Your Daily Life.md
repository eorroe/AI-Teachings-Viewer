# 101 Ways To Use AI In Your Daily Life

## Overview

This AI teaching is a curated collection of 101 practical, actionable ways to integrate AI into everyday life across eight categories: general productivity, work productivity, day-to-day life, personal finances, learning, career and business, relationships, and miscellaneous use cases. It exists to help users move beyond basic chatbot interactions and use AI — through chatbots, agents, specialized apps, and vibe coding tools — to save time, make better decisions, reduce friction, and improve the quality of both personal and professional outputs. The guidance is intentionally tool-agnostic: text-based tasks, analysis, and writing work with ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude; research and multi-source answers work with Perplexity; learning tasks work with Notebook LM; agent workflows work with N8N or OpenAI's Agents SDK; and app building works with vibe coding platforms.

## When to Follow These AI Teachings

- When you want to offload routine cognitive work — such as summarizing slides, organizing a messy to-do list, or extracting data from a 100-page Portable Document Format (PDF) — to AI so you can focus on higher-value thinking.
- When you are switching between workstreams, tasks, or projects and need AI to bookmark your place and generate a summary and action items so you can resume instantly.
- When you are making a decision that benefits from structured frameworks — such as evaluating a startup idea with Alex Hormozi's four-part Million Dollar Offer framework, prioritizing tasks with an Eisenhower matrix, or challenging assumptions with first principles thinking.
- When you are learning a new subject, preparing for an interview, or studying a language and want on-demand tutoring, worked examples, flashcards, or a custom study plan.
- When you are managing personal finances, building a budget tracker, planning for retirement, or optimizing insurance and tax decisions and need a second brain to organize and analyze the data.
- When you are building or prototyping something — a website, an automation, a presentation, a contract, or a full AI agent workflow — and want to reduce the time from idea to working output.
- When you want to practice a difficult conversation, negotiation, or speech using AI voice roleplay before the real interaction.
- When you need help identifying an object from a photo, troubleshooting a household problem, or getting step-by-step guidance on how to use a software tool you are not familiar with.

## Steps

### Step 1: Identify the category of task you want AI to help with

Before using AI, classify your task into one of the eight categories from this video: general productivity, work productivity, daily life, personal finances, learning, career and business, relationships, or miscellaneous. This helps you select the right tool and prompt approach. For example, if your task is "summarize a work meeting," it falls under work productivity. If it is "plan meals for the week," it falls under daily life.

### Step 2: Select the right tool for the task

Use a chatbot (ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude) for text-based, analytical, and writing tasks. Use Perplexity when you need search-powered research and curated answers from multiple sources. Use Notebook LM for learning-focused tasks like creating podcasts, flashcards, and study plans from uploaded documents. Use N8N (no-code) or OpenAI's Agents SDK (code) for building agent workflows and automations. Use a vibe coding tool (Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Firebase Studio, Cursor, Windsurf, or Claude Code) when you want to build a functional app, automation, or dashboard.

### Step 3: Take a photo or upload the relevant file

For visual or document-based tasks, take a clear photo of the object, slide, interface, receipt, or document, or upload the specific file type needed for your task (PDF, video, audio, spreadsheet) directly into the AI tool. When uploading a video for transcription or analysis, use Google AI Studio, which can process video and generate transcripts. When analyzing a large PDF, paste or upload it and ask the AI to extract only the specific information you need rather than summarizing the whole document.

### Step 4: Write a specific, structured prompt

Avoid vague prompts. Use concrete details, constraints, and frameworks. For example, instead of asking "what should I invest in?", specify your location, currency, and constraints: "What kind of financial assets should I invest in if I am a Canadian currently living in Hong Kong and making US dollars?" When brainstorming, use the "answers leveling" technique: ask the AI to generate ideas at four increasing levels of depth — Level 1 (obvious, widely-known ideas), Level 2 (more specific ideas most people miss), Level 3 (highly creative and specific ideas requiring niche knowledge), Level 4 (expert-level ideas that only a specialist would generate) — and aim for Level 3 or Level 4. For writing tasks, specify the desired tone change — such as "rewrite this email from casual to professional" — and ask for edits on grammar, clarity, and tone.

### Step 5: Use structured frameworks to guide specialized responses

When evaluating complex ideas or decisions, apply a named framework rather than asking a general question. For startup or business ideas, use Alex Hormozi's four-part Million Dollar Offer framework. For task prioritization, use the Eisenhower matrix (urgent and important, important but not urgent, urgent but not important, neither). To challenge assumptions you hold about your habits or goals, use first principles thinking and explicitly ask the AI to test whether each assumption is actually true.

### Step 6: Build automations and custom tools with vibe coding or agents

For repetitive tasks, build a custom tool instead of paying for a specialized app. With an agent Application Programming Interface (API) key, you can scrape YouTube comments, process incoming data, and route it to other tools with minimal configuration. If you are comfortable with code, vibe code a cron job to update files or run programs on a schedule, vibe code a Gmail script to send bulk emails, or vibe code a custom Pomodoro timer, finance tracker, or prompt library app. For no-code agent workflows, use N8N to connect Perplexity research with Google Sheets, databases, email, or any other tool in a pipeline. For code-based agents, use OpenAI's Agents SDK for lead generation, customer service bots, database querying, report generation, invoice management, and survey management.

### Step 7: Build recurring reporting and dashboard workflows

For ongoing tracking needs — such as personal expenses, financial dashboards, or key performance metrics — create a recurring workflow. Send receipt photos or text through WhatsApp to an AI agent, which categorizes each item and compiles it into a database. Request daily, weekly, and monthly spending reports from the agent. For more advanced financial management, ask Claude to create an interactive dashboard for tracking income, expenses, and KPIs that you can check at any time.

### Step 8: Use AI voice for thinking, practicing, and real-time narration

Use the voice functionality in your AI chatbot to ramble through problems, brainstorm out loud, or think through business decisions conversationally. Roleplay difficult scenarios — such as negotiating a salary, having a tough conversation with your boss, or practicing a speech — using voice mode so the interaction feels real, according to the speaker. For real-time guidance while using software, use Google AI Studio's real-time streaming feature: share your screen and ask the AI to direct you through each step of a task, such as removing an object from a photo in Photoshop. For real-time narration of a live event or video you do not understand — such as a football game — ask the AI to narrate what is happening as it occurs.

## Examples

### Example 1: Identifying an object and troubleshooting a household problem

Take a photo of an unknown flower, mushroom, or shoe and ask the AI to identify it and tell you whether it is safe to eat or wear. For a household issue such as a leaking toilet, take a photo of the toilet and the control levers and ask the AI which lever to move and in which direction to stop the leak. For device troubleshooting, upload the device manual as a PDF and ask the AI for step-by-step guidance specific to your model and issue.

### Example 2: Summarizing a presentation or lecture from slides or video

During a meeting or lecture, take photos of the slides with your phone. Upload the photos to ChatGPT and ask it to compile and summarize all of them into a single set of notes. If the presentation was recorded as a video, upload it to Google AI Studio. The AI will generate a transcript and you can then ask it to extract key points, action items, or any other specific output. For YouTube videos, download the video file or use a tool that accepts YouTube URLs, then upload it to the AI tool for transcription, summarization, study plan generation, or question answering about the content.

### Example 3: Building a personal expense tracking automation

Every time you make a purchase, take a photo of the receipt or type the item and amount. Send it through WhatsApp to an AI agent that you have configured with an API key. The agent extracts the item name, category, and cost from your message, categorizes each transaction (for example: groceries, dining, transport), and writes it to a Google Sheet or database. At the end of each day, week, and month, ask the agent to generate a spending report with totals by category. If you want to reduce grocery costs, ask the agent to flag food-related purchases and show you the total across the reporting period so you can adjust your behavior.

### Example 4: Creating a custom study plan for a new subject

Use Perplexity to research the topic and curate content from multiple sources. Ask the AI to organize the content into a structured study plan with specific milestones and resources. Use Notebook LM to upload your study notes, then ask it to generate Anki flashcards for spaced repetition, create a podcast episode covering the material for passive listening, and generate mock quizzes to test your knowledge. For language learning, use Notebook LM to generate voice content at your target difficulty level, and use the chatbot's voice feature to practice listening and speaking.

### Example 5: Vibe coding a custom app instead of paying for a subscription

If you need a Pomodoro timer, a finance tracker, or a prompt library app, open a vibe coding tool (Bolt for beginners, Replit or Firebase Studio for intermediate users, Cursor, Windsurf, or Claude Code for advanced users). Describe the app you want in plain language: "Build a Pomodoro timer that tracks completed sessions, shows a daily total, and stores data in local storage." The AI generates the code, you test it, and iterate with follow-up prompts. For consulting demos or client-facing prototypes, the same workflow applies: describe the workflow or data model, generate the app, and refine it in minutes rather than hours of manual coding.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Use a small set of foundational tools — ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude for chatbots; Perplexity for search and research; Notebook LM for learning; N8N for no-code agents; and a vibe coding tool matched to your skill level — rather than paying for multiple narrow subscriptions.
- ✅ Write specific, context-rich prompts with concrete constraints, desired outputs, and formats. Include your location, currency, constraints, and preferences whenever they are relevant to the answer.
- ✅ Use structured frameworks — Alex Hormozi's Million Dollar Offer framework, the Eisenhower matrix, first principles thinking — when evaluating ideas, prioritizing tasks, or making decisions, rather than asking general open-ended questions.
- ✅ Take photos of slides, objects, receipts, and interfaces rather than trying to describe them in text. Visual input gives the AI far more accurate context and produces better results.
- ✅ Use Deep Research mode for decisions that matter a lot to you and where you want to dig deeper — such as financial planning, career moves, or comparing complex products and services — because these tools actively search and cite sources rather than relying solely on pre-trained knowledge.
- ✅ Use answers leveling (Levels 1 through 4) for brainstorming to move from generic ideas to highly specific, actionable ones. Target Level 3 or Level 4 for useful output.
- ✅ Build custom tools and automations through vibe coding instead of paying for narrow subscription apps. A custom Pomodoro timer, finance tracker, or prompt library can be built in minutes and tailored exactly to your needs.
- ✅ Use AI voice for brainstorming, roleplaying difficult conversations, and practicing negotiation so you can think out loud and get conversational feedback without the friction of typing.
- ✅ For ongoing tracking workflows — expenses, KPIs, time — set up an automated pipeline that writes to a database and generates recurring reports rather than manually re-prompting the AI every day.

## Keep In Mind

- This video covers 101 distinct use cases across eight life areas. You do not need to adopt all of them. Start with use cases that solve your most pressing problems right now — such as summarizing documents, brainstorming with answers leveling, or automating expense tracking — and expand from there once you are comfortable.
- Getting strong results from ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude with strong prompt engineering will serve you better than subscribing to specialized products you barely use.
- Answers leveling works for any brainstorming task, not just business ideas. Use it for meal planning, trip planning, content ideas, or any situation where you want creative and specific output rather than generic suggestions.
- Vibe coding tools have different entry points: Lovable and Bolt are designed for beginners with no coding experience, while the creator categorizes Cursor, Windsurf, and Claude Code as tools for users with some coding background. Choose the tool that matches your current skill level rather than the most advanced option.
- When uploading a video to Google AI Studio for transcription and analysis, the platform can process any video — including YouTube videos — and generate transcripts, summaries, and structured outputs from the content.
- The Perplexity API allows you to run research workflows in bulk with no code: upload a Google Sheet with a list of items to research, have Perplexity summarize each one, and pipe the results to other tools for further processing.
- Perplexity, Notebook LM, N8N, Manus, and OpenAI's Agents SDK are separate tools from the core chatbots. Each has a distinct use case, and mixing them strategically — such as using Perplexity for research and piping results into N8N for automation — multiplies their value.
- AI-generated contracts and legal documents are not a substitute for professional legal advice. The creator notes that AI serves as a starting point for contract drafting, not final legal counsel.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not upload sensitive personal documents — such as tax returns, government IDs, medical records, or legal contracts containing personal information — to AI tools unless you have reviewed the tool's data handling policy and confirmed that your data will not be used for model training or retained longer than necessary.
- When using an AI agent with an API key, restrict the agent's permissions to only the services and data sources it needs. Do not give an agent broad access to your email, financial accounts, or cloud storage without understanding what it can do with that access.
- Be cautious when sharing your screen or device interface with AI through real-time streaming features. Close or lock any tabs, apps, or windows containing sensitive information — passwords, banking details, private messages — before sharing your screen.
- When using AI to review resumes, CVs, or other documents that contain personal information about other people, make sure you have the right to share that data with the tool and that the tool will not retain or use it for model training against your wishes.
- Financial dashboards and expense tracking automations that send receipt data to an AI agent should use tools with clear data policies. Understand where your spending data is stored and who can access it before routing sensitive financial information through an automated pipeline.
- For voice roleplay practice — such as negotiation or difficult conversations — be aware that some AI tools can record or retain voice sessions. Review the privacy settings of the tool you are using.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Asking vague or open-ended prompts and receiving generic, low-quality output.
  **Solution:** Add specific constraints, context, and desired formats to every prompt. Instead of "what should I invest in?", write "What kind of financial assets should I invest in if I am a Canadian currently living in Hong Kong and making US dollars?" Include your currency, location, risk tolerance, time horizon, and any other relevant constraint.

- **Problem:** Using a general chatbot for tasks that require live, sourced, or curated information from multiple sources, leading to outdated or incomplete answers.
  **Solution:** Switch to Perplexity for any task that requires research, comparison, or synthesis across multiple sources — such as evaluating tech careers, comparing medical models, or researching in-demand skills. Perplexity actively searches and cites sources in real time.

- **Problem:** Repeating the same manual workflow every day instead of building an automation, leading to wasted time and inconsistent outputs.
  **Solution:** When you catch yourself doing the same task more than twice, stop and build a tool or automation. Use vibe coding for simple custom apps, an AI agent with an API key for data processing pipelines, or N8N for connecting multiple services. The initial setup takes minutes; the time saved compounds daily.

- **Problem:** Relying on AI voice roleplay without preparing specific scenarios, leading to generic and unhelpful practice sessions.
  **Solution:** Before starting a voice roleplay session, define the exact scenario, your goal, and the persona the AI should play. For a salary negotiation, specify your current salary, the raise amount you are targeting, the objections you expect, and ask the AI to roleplay your manager. Treat it like a structured rehearsal, not a casual conversation.

- **Problem:** Using AI to generate contracts, legal documents, or business plans without any review, treating the output as final and legally binding.
  **Solution:** Treat all AI-generated legal and business documents as first drafts only. Review every clause, verify compliance with your local jurisdiction, and consult a qualified professional — a lawyer, accountant, or business advisor — before relying on any AI-generated document for a real transaction or commitment.

- **Problem:** Reading long transcripts or summaries passively instead of converting the content into an active learning format, leading to low retention.
  **Solution:** After generating a transcript or summary from a video, ask the AI to immediately convert the content into an active learning format: flashcards (Anki format), a quiz, a podcast script for passive listening, or an infographic. Active engagement with the material dramatically improves retention compared to passive reading.
